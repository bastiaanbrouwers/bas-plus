problem: |-
  The application for Principal Brand Designer asked me to add something with AI. A link to a deck felt like a missed chance. Ramp's brand is at its best when it makes the boring parts of finance fun, like the receipts that fly out of the cursor on ramp.design.
solution: |-
  A small match game built on those receipts. They slide down a dot floor toward you, and you tap or drag each one onto its card transaction before it lands. Round 1 helps you with colour. Round 2 takes the colour away. Round 3 adds twins: the same amount with a different reference, and the same reference at a different price.

  Designed in Figma, built in Claude, in one afternoon. Best on your phone.
---

Everything in it comes from Ramp's own brand. The receipts and the yellow from ramp.design. The dot grid from the ramp.com homepage, turned into a floor in perspective. The tags copy the live counter in the ramp.com hero.

The copy is where it gets a little quirky. Streaks, misses, twins and the month-end clock all get their own comment. And William keeps an eye on your books.
